LEARN TO SORT WASTE CORRECTLY

What is Waste Segregation? 🌿

Waste segregation means separating waste into different categories so that each type can be treated, recycled or disposed of properly.

♻️ What is Waste Segregation?

Waste segregation is the process of separating waste based on its type and nature. Instead of mixing all garbage together, we sort it into different categories such as wet waste, dry waste, hazardous waste and e-waste.

Proper segregation makes recycling easier, reduces pollution and helps keep our environment clean.

Types of Waste

Understanding each type helps us choose the correct disposal method.

🍌

Wet / Organic Waste

Waste that comes from food, plants and other natural materials. This waste can usually decompose naturally.

Examples:
  • Fruit and vegetable peels
  • Food leftovers
  • Tea leaves
  • Garden waste
💡 Tip: Organic waste can be composted to create useful manure.
📦

Dry Waste

Dry waste includes materials that are generally recyclable, reusable or need separate processing.

Examples:
  • Paper and cardboard
  • Plastic bottles
  • Glass jars
  • Metal cans
💡 Tip: Keep recyclable items clean and dry before disposal.
🔋

Hazardous Waste

Hazardous waste may contain harmful materials and should not be mixed with normal household waste.

Examples:
  • Batteries
  • Paint and chemicals
  • Medicines
  • Cleaning products
⚠️ Tip: Use authorised collection or disposal services.
📱

E-Waste

Electronic waste includes old or damaged electronic devices and their accessories.

Examples:
  • Old mobile phones
  • Chargers and cables
  • Computers
  • Electronic accessories
💡 Tip: Take e-waste to an authorised e-waste collection centre.
WHY IT MATTERS

Why is Waste Segregation Important?

Separating waste at the source is one of the easiest ways to improve waste management.

♻️
Improves Recycling

Clean and separated materials are easier to recycle.

🌍
Reduces Pollution

Proper disposal prevents harmful waste from contaminating the environment.

🌱
Supports Composting

Organic waste can be converted into useful compost.

🏭
Reduces Landfill Waste

Recycling and composting reduce the amount of waste sent to landfills.

How to Segregate Waste at Home

Follow these simple steps every day.

1

Keep Separate Bins

Use different containers for wet waste, dry waste and special waste.

2

Sort Waste Daily

Separate waste when you throw it away instead of mixing everything together.

3

Clean Recyclables

Empty and clean bottles, jars and containers before placing them with dry recyclable waste.

4

Handle Special Waste Safely

Keep batteries, chemicals and electronic waste separate and use appropriate collection facilities.
